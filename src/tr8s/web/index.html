        What to switch on, on the TR-8S
        
      

      
Some things live on the panel and cannot be set over
        USB. Each row says whether it is working right now.


      

        
        

          Follow the pattern you select
          
[UTILITY] → turn [VALUE] to MIDI: Tx Prog Chg
            → [ENTER] → ON

          

        

      


      

        
        

          Follow the A–H variation, and steps entered on the panel
          
The machine sends nothing for either — verified
            with every Tx setting on. So the studio listens: while a
            pattern plays, its notes light the grid live and identify
            the variation within a bar. Tx EditData = ON also sends the
            beat counter (CC 2), which pins the bar phase exactly. While
            stopped there is nothing to hear; presses made then show up
            on the next START.

          

        

      


      

        
        

          Follow knobs and faders
          
[UTILITY] → MIDI: Tx EditData → ON.
            Then every TUNE, DECAY, CTRL and fader, plus accent, reverb,
            delay, master FX and shuffle, moves on screen as you move it.
            The manual's "Using the TR-8S as a MIDI Controller" also says to
            set SOUND: Local Sw to SURFACE — do not, that
            one silences the internal sound engine.

          

        

      


      

        
        

          Hear the melodies
          
MOTION [ON] must be lit, and
            Coarse Tune assigned to that instrument's CTRL knob
            (hold [CTRL SELECT], press the instrument, pick Coarse Tune)

          
cannot be checked from here — panel only

        

      


      

        
        

          Per-pattern tempo, shuffle and kit
          
[UTILITY] → GENERAL → set
            TempoSrc / ShuffleSrc / KitSel to PTN.
            On SYSTEM the panel wins and what a pattern stores is ignored.

          
cannot be checked from here — panel only

        

      


      

        
        

          Keep those settings after a power cycle
          
If [UTILITY] → GENERAL: Auto Save is
            OFF, press [WRITE] once

          
cannot be checked from here — panel only
